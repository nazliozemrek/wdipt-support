FAQ

How do I log something with Siri?

Say “Hey Siri, log something in Where Did I Put That”, then answer in one sentence, like “my passport in the bedroom, bottom drawer”. To find it: “Hey Siri, where is my passport in Where Did I Put That”. Open the app once after installing so Siri learns the phrases.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for WDIPT?. Pick a size and tap Add Widget. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area.

Which iPhones support the 3D Finder?

The 3D Finder uses the LiDAR scanner, available on iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models. It's experimental and works best in the same room, with similar light to when you saved the pin.

Will I lose my items if I get a new iPhone?

Your items are stored on your iPhone and included in your iPhone backups. Restore a backup to your new iPhone and they come with you. Deleting the app deletes its items.

Can I turn off Face ID?

Yes. Open Settings inside the app (the gear icon) and switch off “Require Face ID”. You'll be asked to confirm it's you first.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

WDIPT? does not collect any data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your items: names, places, spots, notes and history. Stored only on your iPhone, shared with the app's own widget so it can show them.
  • Photos you take or choose for an item. Stored only inside the app.
  • Location: only when you switch on “Save my GPS location” for an item. It's saved with that item on your iPhone and used to show it on a map.
  • 3D Finder room scans: when you save a 3D location, a map of the surrounding room is stored with the item on your iPhone so the pin can be found again. It's deleted when the item moves or is deleted.

Services from Apple

  • Face ID: handled entirely by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never sees your face data.
  • Siri: what you say to Siri is processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app only receives the item and place you name.
  • Backups: like most apps, your items are included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on. These are controlled by your Apple account, not by us.

Permissions

The app asks for the camera (photos and 3D scans), photo library (choosing a photo), location (optional GPS spot) and Face ID. Each is optional and only used for the feature you choose. You can change them any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete an item in the app to remove it, its photo, location and 3D scan. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
